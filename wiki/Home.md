# Casa Elida · Anuncios

**Español:** Guías para usar e instalar los anuncios de la segunda pantalla de caja. Empiece por [[Uso diario|Uso-diario]], [[Instalación|Instalacion]] o [[Solución de problemas|Solucion-de-problemas]].

**English:** Guides for using and installing signage on the cashier PC's second display. Start with [[Daily use|Daily-use]], [[Installation]] or [[Troubleshooting]].

The source code, Windows PowerShell 5.1 checks, and release artifacts are in the [public ce-signage repository](https://github.com/j3w1/ce-signage). The software and this wiki are licensed under [[MIT|License]].

The controls use a normal console window. VLC playback remains fullscreen on monitor 2. The planned window-state watchdog is not part of v3.4.
