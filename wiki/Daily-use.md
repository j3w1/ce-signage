# Daily use

1. Put images or videos in `C:\Users\windows\Desktop\anuncios`. The system keeps the originals and prepares private playback copies.
2. Open `ANUNCIOS.cmd` on the desktop. The menu shows the current state, ready and skipped files, and a suggested next step.
3. Choose **1 Start**, **2 Stop**, **3 Detailed status and notices**, or **0 Exit**. Stopping from the menu requires typing `S` to confirm.
4. The existing `START ANUNCIOS.cmd`, `STOP ANUNCIOS.cmd`, and `STATUS ANUNCIOS.cmd` shortcuts still work without administrator access.

Images display for 10 seconds; videos play for their full duration. Audio is disabled. Playback is randomized and loops. After adding, replacing, or deleting files, allow time for preparation. Corrupt files are skipped while valid ads continue.

Monitor 1 stays available to the cashier. If monitor 2 disconnects, signage stops instead of moving onto monitor 1. v3.4 does not recover automatically from manually minimizing VLC; use Start/Status and contact the administrator if the issue persists.
